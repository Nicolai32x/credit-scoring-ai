# Development data exploration

Generated by [notebook 01](../notebooks/01_exploration.ipynb) on 20 October 2026.
All observations below concern the **800 development cases**, not the 200
held-out test cases. There are no trained-model or model-performance results yet.

## Main descriptive findings

- Labels: 560 good risks (70%) and 240 bad risks (30%). An always-good prediction
  gives 70% accuracy but no bad-risk recall; this is a reference calculation,
  not an evaluated model.
- No empty input values, repeated seven-feature profiles or unexpected category
  codes were found in development data. Combined unknown categories remain:
  savings code A65 accounts for 144 cases (18%).
- Credit amount: median 2,317 historical DM; mean 3,189.59 DM; range 276-15,945 DM.
  The upper tail is long. Large values are retained rather than assumed erroneous.
- Duration: median 18 months, mean 20.77 months, range 4-60 months.
- Small loan-purpose groups include retraining (5 cases), other (6), domestic
  appliances (10) and repairs (18). Their observed label shares are unstable.
- Numeric scales differ and categorical codes are identifiers. Encoding and
  numeric scaling will be learned inside training folds in the next step.

The notebook also shows category-level bad-risk shares with denominators and
small-group flags. These are descriptive associations, not causal effects,
validated individual probabilities, or decision rules. A group size of 20 is
only a display flag and does not establish statistical reliability.
